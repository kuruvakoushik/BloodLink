Know before you donate

Every donation starts with a conversation.

Blood donation eligibility, screening, and transfusion matching must be assessed by qualified healthcare professionals at a licensed blood bank.

BloodLink helps people discover and coordinate. It does not provide medical advice or determine eligibility or compatibility.

Emergency? Contact a hospital or licensed blood bank immediately. Do not wait for a response on this platform.
